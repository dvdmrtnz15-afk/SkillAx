# Causal Reality Contract v0.1

## Purpose

Causal realism treats visible variation as the result of a world history. It does not reward random messiness.

## Node kinds

- `cause`: prior state or event such as heat, hand motion, depleted ink, wind, or impact.
- `transition`: intermediate physical or temporal state such as sweating, drying, deformation, or tool starvation.
- `effect`: observable residue such as a smear, wrinkle, highlight, blur, stain, scratch-out, or displaced object.
- `observation`: capture-chain consequence when a camera/sensor/observer changes how world state is recorded.

## Invariants

1. Every consequential visible `effect` requires at least one incoming causal edge.
2. The causal graph must be acyclic for a single compiled episode.
3. Counterfactual removal of a cause must enumerate downstream states expected to change.
4. Imperfection density is bounded; sterile and theatrically distressed outputs are rejected.
5. Evaluations are evidence only. They grant no publish, merge, deploy, identity-mutation, or other effect authority.
6. Personality, diagnosis, identity, honesty, or mental state must never be inferred from handwriting or appearance.

## Product boundary

Character-specific motor signatures, handwriting parameters, biographies, relationship canon, and proprietary visual references remain product-owned state. This skill consumes those constraints when supplied but does not embed them.
