# Human-adaptive scorecard

Do not maximize every dimension. Score only what the situation requires. Note conflicts instead of forcing a perfect grid.

## Functional

| Dimension | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Clarity | Can the recipient quickly understand the purpose? |
| Actionability | Is the next step obvious and easy? |
| Conciseness | Is every sentence earning its place? |
| Outcome alignment | Does the message support the intended result? |

## Relational

| Dimension | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Warmth | Does the recipient feel recognized as a person? |
| Approachability | Does responding feel easy? |
| Respect | Is position and effort appropriately recognized? |
| Psychological safety | Can the recipient engage without unnecessary anxiety? |

## Leadership

| Dimension | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Confidence | Does the sender sound grounded? |
| Leadership presence | Does the sender appear intentional and credible? |
| Mentorship signal | If relevant, is support present without superiority? |
| Boundary integrity | Does the message avoid overfamiliarity or unintended pressure? |

## Voice

| Dimension | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Authenticity | Does it sound like the sender? |
| Recipient compatibility | Does it fit the recipient's communication range? |
| Corporate stiffness | Is the message more institutional than necessary? |
| Generational stylization | Do slang, emoji, or trend language overpower the situation? |

## Known tradeoffs

- Max warmth can break boundary integrity.
- Max brevity can reduce psychological safety.
- Max formality can reduce approachability.
- Max authority can weaken mentorship.

Report 3–7 relevant scores in plain language (strong / adequate / weak) plus one tradeoff you accepted on purpose.
